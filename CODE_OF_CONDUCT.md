# Code of conduct

Discuss technical claims with evidence and respect. Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and disclosure of another person's private information. Maintain clear attribution and distinguish proposed capabilities from measured results.

Report conduct concerns privately to protoscience@anulum.li. The maintainer reviews the specific conduct and may require corrections, remove inappropriate material or restrict participation. Reports are handled with reasonable confidentiality; public issues must omit sensitive material.
